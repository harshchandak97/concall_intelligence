Notes

1. Borderline items considered and excluded
- Double revenues every 3–4 years (pg 4, 11) — long-term aspiration, not verifiable within 4 quarters. Excluded.
- INR 10,000 cr revenue → ~INR 450 cr PAT, 18–20% post-tax ROCE (pg 12–13) — tied to the 3–4 year doubling; outside the window. Excluded.
- EV business profitability in FY28; Romania breakeven in FY28 (pg 6, 11) — genuine commitment events but ~5–8 quarters out. Excluded.
- "EBITDA levels will remain constant around 11%" (pg 12) — steady-state comment in a long-term context, no near-term trackable change. Excluded.
- Term-loan repayment of ~INR 103 cr in FY27 (pg 13) — contractual repayment schedule, not performance guidance. Excluded.
- Telematics/technology transfer "to the market within the next 12 months" (pg 14) — conditional, no number. Excluded.

2. Numbers originating in the analyst's question
- id:5: the INR 800 cr figure came from Ashutosh Tiwari's question; management corrected it to ~INR 700–750 cr. The INR 468 cr base and INR 750 cr target are management's own numbers.

3. Timeline derivations
- Call is Q4 FY26, dated 25 May 2026. "This / current / upcoming financial year" = FY27. "Quarter 2 / Quarter 3" = Q2 / Q3 FY27.

4. Metric clarifications
- id:2: guidance_value "0.25" is a delta (improvement of 0.25 percentage points), not an absolute EBITDA margin target. Applied per the delta edge-case rule in the extraction prompt.
- id:2: credibility_scorable is false because the 0.25pp target applies to existing projects only; Screener.in shows consolidated OPM%, which will include new-project drag, making the guided delta not directly matchable.
- id:6, id:7, id:8: These are profitability milestone events (existing projects reaching EBT breakeven), not new plant commissioning events. metric changed from commissioning_event to other_ebt_breakeven.
- id:7 nuance: management guides EBT turnaround from Q3 FY27 but expects full-year FY27 to still show EBT-level losses for this unit. The tracked outcome is onset of turnaround, not full-year profitability.
- id:5 value inconsistency: "2.5x of INR 342 cr investment" implies ~INR 855 cr but management's stated rupee figure is "around INR 750 crores" (described as 2x–2.5x of the INR 468 cr revenue base). Used the explicit rupee figure. Verify before locking.
