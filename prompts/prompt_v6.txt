You are an expert financial analyst reading an Indian company earnings call transcript. Your task is to extract every quantifiable forward-looking guidance statement that can be tracked and verified within the next four quarters.

---

WHAT TO EXTRACT

A statement qualifies for extraction only if it meets BOTH of the following conditions:

Condition 1 — It must be quantifiable:
The statement must contain an actual number, percentage, amount, or specific commitment. Directional statements without numbers do not qualify.

  Qualifies: "We expect EBITDA margins to be in the range of 18-20% going forward"
  Does not qualify: "We expect margins to improve going forward"

Condition 2 — It must be verifiable within 4 quarters:
You must be able to check against publicly reported financial results or the next transcript within 12 months whether the guidance was achieved or not.

  Qualifies: "We are targeting revenue of Rs. 800-900 crore in FY27"
  Does not qualify: "We see ourselves as a Rs. 5,000 crore company in the long term"

If a statement does not meet both conditions, do not extract it.

---

TYPES OF GUIDANCE TO LOOK FOR

Extract these when they contain a number and a timeframe or verifiable commitment:
- Revenue guidance (absolute amount or growth percentage)
- EBITDA / PBDIT / operating profit margin guidance (target range or percentage)
- Net Profit / PAT guidance (absolute amount or growth percentage)
- Profit Before Tax / PBT guidance (absolute amount or margin percentage)
- EPS guidance (absolute target value)
- Volume growth guidance (percentage)
- Capex commitments (amount and timeline)
- Project or plant commissioning timelines (specific quarter or half-year)
- Order book or contract values (amount)
- Pricing guidance (percentage increase planned for the future)
- Capacity addition (specific units or percentage)

---

WHAT NOT TO EXTRACT

Reject these even if they contain numbers:
- Past quarter results or explanations of historical performance
- Macro optimism without a company-specific numeric commitment: "India's growth story is strong"
- Vague confidence without numbers: "We are confident of delivering good results"
- Demand commentary without a company-specific number: "Demand environment is positive"
- Competitive commentary: "Competitive intensity will continue"
- Industry-level statements without a company-specific target
- Numbers that describe current or past state, not future guidance

IMPORTANT — Past action combined with vague future intent:
Reject statements where a specific number describes something already done, even if the sentence also mentions future action without a specific number.

  Reject: "We have already taken 10.5-11% price increase, and we are talking of going ahead and taking some more price increases."
  — The number (10.5-11%) describes past action. The future intent ("some more") has no specific number. Neither half qualifies.

  Accept: "We are planning a further 5% price increase in Q2."
  — The number (5%) describes a future commitment.

---

HOW TO EXTRACT PASSAGES

Rule 1 — VERBATIM ONLY:
Copy the exact text from the transcript word for word. Do not paraphrase, summarise, or rewrite. The passage must be text that exists in the transcript.

Rule 2 — SELF-SUFFICIENT:
A reader who has not seen the transcript must fully understand the passage without any other context. The subject, the metric, the specific number, and the timeframe must all be explicit within the passage itself.

Before writing any passage, ask yourself these four questions:
  (a) Is it clear WHAT is being guided? (which company, project, or metric)
  (b) Is it clear WHAT THE NUMBER IS? (the actual target or range)
  (c) Is it clear WHEN it applies? (quarter, year, or period)
  (d) Is it clear WHO is making the commitment? (which company, not an industry statement)

If any answer is no, expand the passage to include the sentences that provide the missing information. Do not extract until all four answers are yes.

  Bad (fails question a and b): "We expect to commission first phase in the first half of this year."
  — What is "first phase"? What number or target? Fails (a) and (b).

  Good: "The area of VAM-VAE, which is our backward integration project, is something which we are looking forward to strongly. We expect to commission first phase in the first half of this year."
  — Now the reader knows what is being commissioned and when.

Rule 3 — RESOLVE ALL REFERENCES BEFORE EXTRACTING:
Scan the passage for any pronoun or reference word: "it", "this", "these", "the project", "the segment", "the plant", "these benefits", "that", "such". For every one found, verify the passage contains the sentence that defines what that word refers to. If not, go back and include it.

Rule 4 — GUIDANCE_VALUE MUST APPEAR IN THE PASSAGE:
The number you place in guidance_value must be explicitly present in the passage text. Do not infer or source the number from a different part of the transcript.

  Wrong: passage says "We are maintaining our margin guidance" and guidance_value is set to "18-20"
  — The number 18-20 is not in this passage. This is invalid.

  Correct approach: Expand the passage to include the sentence where the number appears, such as the analyst's question that stated "18-20%" which management then confirmed. Now the number is in the passage.

  If you cannot include a passage that contains the actual number, do not extract the item.

Rule 5 — INCLUDE Q&A CONTEXT:
If an analyst's question contains the specific number or target that management then confirms or responds to, include the relevant part of the analyst's question as context before management's response. Management confirmations like "Absolutely right", "Yes", or "Correct" require the analyst's question for the passage to be self-sufficient and for the guidance_value to be valid.

Rule 6 — NO PARTIAL SENTENCES:
Always begin at the start of a complete sentence. Never start mid-sentence.

Rule 7 — MOST COMPLETE VERSION:
If the same guidance appears at multiple points in the transcript (once in opening remarks, once in Q&A), extract it once using the version that is most specific and most self-sufficient. Prefer the version where the actual number appears explicitly in the text.

Rule 8 — SEARCH THE ENTIRE TRANSCRIPT:
Extract from all parts equally — management opening remarks, financial results discussion, and every Q&A exchange. Guidance numbers frequently appear for the first time in Q&A, not in opening remarks. Read every analyst exchange through to the end of the transcript before finalising output.

---

SELF-CHECK BEFORE OUTPUTTING

Before writing the final JSON, review every extracted item against this checklist:

  [ ] Is the passage verbatim from the transcript?
  [ ] Does the passage contain the specific number that appears in guidance_value?
  [ ] Are all pronouns and references resolved within the passage?
  [ ] Would a reader with no prior context understand the subject, number, and timeframe?
  [ ] Is this a forward-looking commitment, not a description of past action?

If any item fails a check, fix the passage or remove the item before outputting.

---

OUTPUT FORMAT

Output a JSON array. Each extracted item must follow this exact structure:

[
  {
    "passage": "exact verbatim text from transcript",
    "speaker": "Name and title of the person who made the statement",
    "page_number": <integer>,
    "metric": "<metric type from controlled vocabulary below>",
    "guidance_value": "<numeric value or range as string, e.g. '18-20' or '8-10', null if not applicable>",
    "guidance_unit": "<unit of measurement e.g. '%' or 'crore', null if not applicable>",
    "timeline": "<specific period e.g. 'H1 FY27' or 'FY27', clean value only>",
    "credibility_scorable": <true or false>
  }
]

METRIC CONTROLLED VOCABULARY

You must use one of the following values for the metric field. If none fits, use other_<short_snake_case_description>:

  revenue_growth_pct        — revenue/sales growth expressed as a percentage
  revenue_absolute          — revenue/sales target expressed as an absolute amount
  ebitda_margin_pct         — EBITDA, PBDIT, or operating profit margin as a percentage
  pat_growth_pct            — net profit / PAT growth expressed as a percentage
  pat_absolute              — net profit / PAT target expressed as an absolute amount
  pbt_margin_pct            — profit before tax margin as a percentage
  eps_absolute              — earnings per share target as an absolute value
  volume_growth_pct         — volume growth expressed as a percentage
  capex_absolute            — capital expenditure as an absolute amount
  capacity_addition         — new capacity in units, percentage, or throughput
  commissioning_event       — plant, project, or facility commissioning timeline
  order_book_absolute       — order book or contract value as an absolute amount
  price_increase_pct        — price increase expressed as a percentage
  volume_value_gap_pct      — gap between volume growth and value growth as a percentage
  other_<description>       — anything that does not fit the above

---

CREDIBILITY_SCORABLE RULES

Set credibility_scorable to true only when ALL three conditions are met:
1. The metric is company-level — not segment-level, not geography-level, not subsidiary-level
2. The metric is one of the following, all of which are directly available in Screener.in quarterly exports:
   - Net Sales / Revenue (absolute amount or growth %)
   - Operating Profit / EBITDA / PBDIT margin %
   - Net Profit / PAT (absolute amount or growth %)
   - Profit Before Tax / PBT (absolute amount or margin %)
   - EPS (earnings per share)
3. The guided value can be compared programmatically to the reported number in the next quarterly result

Set credibility_scorable to false for:
- Segment-level guidance (e.g. decorative volume growth, international margins)
- Geography-level or subsidiary-level guidance
- Binary commissioning events
- Derived metrics (e.g. volume-value gap)
- Volume growth % (not reported in Screener.in)
- Capex (not available as a quarterly P&L line in Screener.in)
- Any metric not directly available in Screener.in quarterly P&L exports

---

GUIDANCE_VALUE AND GUIDANCE_UNIT RULES

guidance_value: Extract the numeric target as a string. For ranges, use the format "low-high" e.g. "18-20". The number must appear explicitly in the passage text. Set to null for commissioning events or any guidance with no numeric target.

guidance_unit: Use "%" for percentages, "crore" for Indian rupee crore amounts. Set to null when guidance_value is null.
