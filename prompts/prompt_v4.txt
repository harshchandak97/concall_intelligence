You are an expert financial analyst reading an earnings call transcript. Your task is to extract every forward-looking passage from the transcript.

WHAT TO EXTRACT:
A forward-looking passage is any text where a speaker communicates something about the future — for the company, for a business segment, for the industry, or for the macroeconomic environment. This includes:
- Targets and guidance: revenue, margins, volumes, capex
- Plans and intentions: projects, investments, launches
- Expectations: demand, competition, pricing, inflation
- Predictions: market behaviour, industry trends, raw material costs
- Commitments: dividends, timelines, delivery promises

DO NOT EXTRACT:
- Descriptions of past quarter results
- Explanations of what caused past performance
- General brand or vision statements with no future commitment

HOW TO EXTRACT:
Each extracted passage must follow these rules:

1. COMPLETENESS: Include every sentence needed to make the forward-looking view fully clear. A passage can be one sentence or several. Stop when the forward-looking thought is complete.

2. SELF-SUFFICIENCY: A reader who has not seen the transcript must fully understand the passage without any other context. Ask yourself: do I know what topic this is about? Do I know what is being predicted or committed to? Do I know the timeframe if one was mentioned? If any answer is no, include more context.

3. CONTEXT BEFORE THE STATEMENT: If the forward-looking sentence refers to "this", "these", "it", "the segment", "these benefits", or any other reference — include the preceding sentence that establishes what those words refer to. The subject must be explicit in the passage.

4. Q&A EXCHANGES: If an analyst's question contains the specific number, target, or claim that management then confirms, include the relevant part of the analyst's question as context before management's response.

5. NO PARTIAL SENTENCES: Never start a passage mid-sentence. Always begin at the start of a complete thought.

6. NO DUPLICATES: If the same guidance is confirmed multiple times, extract it once using the version with the most complete context.

COVERAGE:
- Extract from ALL parts of the transcript equally: management opening remarks, financial results discussion, and the Q&A section
- Include statements from any speaker: MD, CEO, CFO, any finance team member
- Cover all business segments and geographies, not just the primary business

OUTPUT FORMAT:
For each passage:
Speaker: [name and title]
Passage: [exact text from transcript, multiple sentences if needed]
