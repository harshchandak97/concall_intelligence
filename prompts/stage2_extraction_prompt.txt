You are reading a segment of an Indian company earnings call transcript. Your job is to extract forward-looking guidance statements — nothing else.

---

WHAT QUALIFIES

Track A — Numeric guidance: management gives a specific number or range AND a timeframe.
Track B — Binary commitment: management commits to a specific verifiable event (plant commissioning, go-live, breakeven) AND a timeframe. A worded timeframe ("second half of this year") qualifies.

If a statement has no number AND no verifiable event, do not extract it.
If a statement has a number but no timeframe, do not extract it.

---

PASSAGE RULES

1. Copy verbatim from the transcript. Do not paraphrase or add words.

2. The passage must be self-sufficient — a reader with no other context must understand:
   (a) what is being guided, (b) the specific number, (c) the timeframe, (d) that it is company-specific.
   If any of these is missing, expand the passage to include the preceding sentence that supplies it.

3. If management confirms or accepts a number from an analyst's question, include the analyst's question
   in the passage. Any passage beginning with "Yes", "Correct", "Absolutely", "Right", "Sure", or
   similar confirmation words must include the analyst's question before it.

4. The number in guidance_value must be visible as digits in the passage text.

---

METRIC DESCRIPTION

Describe what is being guided in plain English. Do not use any label or classification.
Examples:
  "Company-level EBITDA margin target for FY27"
  "EV segment revenue target derived from doubling current ₹20 crore base"
  "New VAM-VAE plant commissioning event"

---

EXAMPLES

Extract:
  Passage: "We expect EBITDA margins of 18-20% in FY27."
  → metric_description: "Company-level EBITDA margin target for FY27"
    guidance_value: "18-20", guidance_unit: "%", timeline: "FY27"

  Passage: "Our EV segment is currently at ₹20 crore and we expect it to double this financial year."
  → metric_description: "EV segment revenue target (derived: double of ₹20 crore = ₹40 crore)"
    guidance_value: "40", guidance_unit: "crore", timeline: "this financial year"

  Passage: "We expect to commission our new VAM-VAE plant in H1 FY27."
  → metric_description: "VAM-VAE plant commissioning event in H1 FY27"
    guidance_value: null, guidance_unit: null, timeline: "H1 FY27"

Do not extract:
  "We are confident of delivering good results." — no number, no timeframe.
  "Demand environment remains positive." — no company-specific commitment.
  "We have already implemented a 10% price increase and plan some further increases." — the number is past; future intent has no specific number.

---

Return an empty items list if no qualifying statements exist in this chunk.
Do not extract past-quarter results, sector commentary, or vague statements.
